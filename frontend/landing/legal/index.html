2. No guarantee of accuracy

Positioning data is derived from third-party market data vendors and computed using models and assumptions described on the methodology page. While Quantro aims for accuracy, data may be delayed, incomplete, or contain errors, and calculated figures (including walls and zero-gamma levels) are theoretical outputs of a model, not guaranteed or verified market outcomes. Quantro makes no warranty, express or implied, as to the accuracy, completeness, or timeliness of any data displayed.

3. Modeling assumptions

In particular, the dealer-short hedging convention used to sign gamma and delta exposure (assuming dealers are net short calls and puts based on typical customer flow) is a standard assumption used broadly across the options positioning-data industry. It is not a verified fact about any specific market maker's actual book, and actual dealer positioning may differ materially from what this convention implies. Implied volatility, greeks, and open interest inputs are themselves vendor-reported figures and subject to that vendor's own data quality and refresh cadence.

4. No guarantee of results

Past patterns in dealer positioning data (such as price historically reacting near a call wall, put wall, or zero-gamma level) do not guarantee similar behavior in the future. Markets are influenced by many factors beyond options positioning. Any decisions made using information from Quantro are made at your own discretion and risk.
