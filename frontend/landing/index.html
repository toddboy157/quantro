Under the hood

From raw chain to dealer wall in four steps

No black box — every number on the dashboard traces back to a real options chain and a documented calculation.

1

Ingest the chain

Pull the live options chain — strikes, open interest, implied vol, greeks — for every underlying you're tracking.

2

Compute exposure

Recompute gamma and delta per contract, sign them by the standard dealer-short convention, and weight by open interest and contract size.

3

Aggregate & locate

Sum exposure by strike and by expiry, find the call wall, put wall, and the zero-gamma crossing via scenario re-pricing.

4

Serve it live

Push the recomputed positioning to your dashboard on a short refresh cycle, with history retained for context.

Important

Quantro shows positioning data only. Dealer walls, GEX/DEX, and the zero-gamma level describe options market structure as it exists right now — they are not buy/sell signals, price targets, or investment advice.

The dealer-short hedging convention is a standard industry assumption, not a verified fact about any specific market maker's book. Always do your own research. Read the full disclaimer →
