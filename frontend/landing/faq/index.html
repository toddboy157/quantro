Frequently asked questions

If something's not covered here, the methodology page goes deeper on the actual calculations.

What is "dealer positioning" data?

It's a read on options market structure derived from open interest and greeks across a live chain — specifically, where market makers' hedging flow is concentrated (the "walls") and where it flips from stabilizing to destabilizing (the "zero-gamma level"). It describes the current state of the options market, not a prediction of where price is headed.

Is this a trading signal or investment advice?

No. Quantro shows positioning data only — dealer walls, GEX, DEX, and the zero-gamma level as they exist right now. None of it is a buy/sell signal, a price target, or investment advice. See the full disclaimer for details.

What's a "call wall" and "put wall"?

The call wall is the strike with the highest positive net gamma exposure; the put wall is the strike with the lowest (most negative). These are strikes where dealer hedging flow is theoretically largest — levels price often gravitates toward, pins against, or bounces from. Full formula on the methodology page.

What's the "zero-gamma level"?

The spot price where total dealer gamma exposure crosses from positive to negative. Above it, dealer hedging tends to dampen volatility; below it, hedging flow tends to amplify whatever move is already happening. Quantro computes it by re-pricing the whole chain across a range of hypothetical spot levels and finding the sign crossing.

How often does the data update?

The dashboard polls every 2 seconds. On the free Delayed plan, the underlying chain data itself is 15-minute delayed (a vendor-side limitation, not a Quantro one); the Live plan uses real-time chain data underneath that same 2-second dashboard refresh.

Which underlyings are covered?

Index and single-name equity options — SPX, SPY, and QQQ alongside individual names like AAPL, NVDA, MSFT, TSLA, and AMZN. The Delayed plan covers up to 3 underlyings at a time; Live and Desk cover the full universe.

Where does the data come from?

Live options chain data (open interest, implied volatility, and greeks) from a licensed market data vendor. Quantro recomputes gamma and delta itself from that raw chain using its own Black-Scholes implementation, so every number on the dashboard is consistent and traceable to a documented formula rather than a vendor's own aggregate.

Why would net GEX or a wall level suddenly jump between ticks?

Open interest is only published once per session by the exchange (real vendors don't refresh it tick-by-tick either) - so most of a wall's movement between ticks comes from spot itself moving, not from OI changing. A bigger jump usually means either an options expiry rolling off, or open interest updating at its normal session cadence.

Is the "dealer short gamma" assumption verified?

No — it's the standard industry convention used across the positioning-data industry (customers are assumed net buyers of options, so dealers who take the other side hedge as if net short). It's a reasonable, widely-used assumption, not a confirmed fact about any specific market maker's actual book.

Do I need an account to use the dashboard?

No signup is required to explore the Delayed plan — just open the dashboard. Live and Desk plans will require an account once billing is wired up (this is currently a prototype build).
